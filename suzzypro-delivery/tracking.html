How it works

Five checkpoints, full visibility

Every SuzzyPro shipment moves through the same tracked stages, from pickup to your door.

1

Package Received

Your item is scanned in at pickup or drop-off.

2

Sorting Facility

Routed to the correct hub for its destination.

3

In Transit

On the move between cities or facilities.

4

Out for Delivery

Loaded onto the final vehicle to your address.
