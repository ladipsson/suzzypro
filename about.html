Built by people who hate late deliveries too.

SuzzyPro Delivery started as a two-rider operation in Lagos traffic. Today we're a nationwide network — but the obsession with getting there on time hasn't changed.

Our story

From two bikes to a 450-driver network

SuzzyPro Delivery began in 2019 when our founder, tired of missed delivery windows on her own e-commerce orders, decided Lagos deserved a courier service that treated deadlines as promises, not estimates.

What started with two riders and a spreadsheet has grown into a technology-backed logistics network spanning 37 cities, serving everyone from solo entrepreneurs shipping their first order to enterprise clients running full warehousing operations.

We're still run by people who've ridden the routes, packed the vans, and answered the "where's my package" calls — which is why reliability isn't a slogan here, it's the whole business model.
